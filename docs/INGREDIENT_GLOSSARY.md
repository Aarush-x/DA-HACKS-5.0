# Ingredient glossary — draft for review (with sources)

Draft only. Not wired into the code. Covers every distinct ingredient across the
24 catalog products, merged where several label names mean the same thing.

**Rating** — suggestion for the Good / Neutral / Limit chips:
- **Good** — adds fiber, whole grain, or useful nutrients.
- **Neutral** — functional; no meaningful nutrition effect at label amounts.
- **Limit** — worth watching across a day (added sugar, sodium, trans-fat sources).

**Evidence** — how well-supported the "why it matters" line is: established · limited · mixed.

**Src** — codes refer to the numbered source list at the bottom. "—" means a
plain functional fact (e.g. "yeast makes dough rise") that doesn't need a citation.

**Amount note for every card:** labels don't list amounts. Use position (main /
secondary / under 2% after the "2% or less" line) plus the product's nutrition
totals instead of guessing.

## Allergens to flag on the card
Wheat · milk (whey, nonfat milk, butter) · soy (soy lecithin, soybean oil) ·
sesame · tree nuts (almond). These are among the 9 major US food allergens (S9, S10).
Always pair with: *"Check the package — this isn't a guarantee against cross-contact."*
